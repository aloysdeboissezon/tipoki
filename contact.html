Contact

The fastest way to reach us is by email.

contact@qwebbo.com

What to write to us about

  • Feedback and ideas.
  • A potential mistake in a question: you can also use the report button during a game.
  • Your data: access, correction or deletion of your account or progress (see the privacy policy).
  • Schools, teachers and partnerships.
  • Lost password: there is no email on accounts, so write to us with your username.
