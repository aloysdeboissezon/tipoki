Our vision

qwebbo started as a way to revise sociology exams in a collaborative and emulative way. Rereading your notes over and over does not work: it drains both attention and energy. Challenging yourself and your friends with trivia, quizzes and competitions turns the logic around. It boosts motivation, sharpens focus and makes time an ally, not a foe.

That is why qwebbo is much more than a quiz: it is a quest. A quest that nods to Hermann Ebbinghaus, the pioneering German psychologist who first described the learning curve. qwebbo helps you learn more effectively and reach your best results. Learn, compete, master, and unlock the opportunities that success in higher education can bring.
