How qwebbo flashcards work

qwebbo turns course notes into flashcards and quizzes. Flip a card, answer, and the questions you miss come back until you know them. It is a free study app that runs in your browser and works offline as an installable app.

Why flashcards and quizzes

Rereading notes feels productive but does not stick. Active recall, answering questions from memory, and spaced repetition are proven to help you remember more. qwebbo adds a game layer so revising is something you want to do.

Play solo, in exam mode, or live with friends using a short game code. Leagues and progress tracking keep you motivated.
